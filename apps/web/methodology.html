Evidence and confidence methodology

The default analyst view includes validated government measurements and issuer disclosures, with targets, BE/RE and valuation estimates visibly labelled. Estimates are excluded from measured totals and highlights. Original BE may serve as the denominator of an explicitly labelled budget comparison only when the entity, budget line, fiscal period, accounting basis, source and observation cutoff match a full-period actual. Targets, valuations and undated context are excluded from derived calculations. Undated disclosures require an explicit context selection. Proxy signals, synthetic scenarios, document indexes and unverified snapshots remain in the source inventory, with explicit readiness labels.

Confidence is a quality signal

Source credibility, observation age, extraction quality, analytical coverage and freshness of retrieval are distinct. A successful download or a recent retrieval does not make an old observation current. High confidence does not certify investment suitability or statistical causality.

  1. Completeness: missingness in the parsed observations, with missing values kept separate from observed zero.
  2. Recency: observation dates and publication dates evaluated separately from when a source was checked or fetched.
  3. Provenance: government, issuer, proxy or model evidence and declared source reliability.
  4. Consistency: schema, unit, range, reconciliation and entity checks. Source-specific validation accompanies generic checks.
  5. Extraction: parser/OCR quality where applicable, including known low-confidence and failed rows.

High / Med / Low badges retain the pipeline quality scores and reasons. Each chart uses the lowest confidence among its contributing sources. The catalog floor also includes gaps and model artifacts; it is not the badge of every chart.

What can be compared

  • Entities: NHAI, MoRTH programmes, state governments, state expressway corporations, concession SPVs and InvIT trusts have different scopes. Location does not establish ownership. Standalone and consolidated accounts are separate.
  • Road classes: National Highways, State Highways and state expressways are explicit classifications. RBI Roads and Bridges expenditure is a broader functional category than State Highways.
  • Time: actual, Budget Estimate (BE), Revised Estimate (RE), targets and year-to-date (YTD) values retain their fiscal/calendar basis and cutoff. Targets are not achieved outcomes. Per-state observation years may differ within a report.
  • Units: original values and units accompany normalized values. Crore, lakh and million rupees are converted explicitly. Route-km, lane-km, bridges counted in numbers, traffic PCU and payment transactions remain distinct.
  • Stock and flow: network length is a stock at a date; construction is a period flow; project portfolio length is the scope of disclosed works. No geographic fallback substitutes one for another.
  • Finance: gross, recoveries and net expenditure are separate rows. Funding, toll receipts, concession consideration, enterprise valuation and distribution per unit are separate measures. DPU can include interest, dividend and capital repayment.
  • Debt: state government debt and guarantees cover all sectors and are separate from highway corporation obligations. Guarantees are contingent exposures, not amounts added to outstanding debt. Contractual undiscounted maturities and balance-sheet carrying values retain their distinct statement bases.
  • Traffic: NPCI NETC is national payment-network data, not NHAI-only vehicle traffic. Transactions are not unique vehicles. Retain source exclusions and changes in acquisition/annual-pass treatment.

Latest published coverage

Research cutoff: 2 October 2026. Each metric scope shows its own latest observation, entity coverage, publication lag, check outcome and next release when explicitly announced. A newly ended September quarter is not treated as published. Unknown dates remain unknown. Final 2024 safety statistics, FY2024–25 GSDP and audited state accounts, and August 2026 monitored-project evidence retain separate source vintages and assurance labels.

The evidence explorer offers all history, latest per entity and common-period selections. GSDP charts choose the period with the widest compatible entity coverage among the three latest observed years, breaking ties toward the latest. State-specific GSDP revision status is not inferred. Three unique compatible monthly flows are required for a quarter; project cumulative expenditure and debt stocks are not quarterly flows.

MoRTH's published national NH length differs from its state sum by 2 km. The inconsistent aggregate remains visible with a calculation exclusion. PAIMANA covers monitored projects of at least ₹150 crore, not the entire road network; its project rows are not added to NHIDCL portfolios without a verified identity crosswalk.

Disclosure tables retain entity, agency, location, road class, metric, unit, reporting period, estimate type, accounting basis, data-as-of, publication date, evidence class and document/table/page citation. CSV exports include all filtered observations and original units. Missing schedules, blocked sources and documents without validated extraction are shown as unavailable; no zero is fabricated.

Historical audit findings retain the audited period and sample. Issuer forecasts and valuations are estimates. Synthetic scenario rows are excluded from analyst totals and are visible only through explicit exploration filters.

Safety and correlation

NH fatalities normalized against a later network snapshot are burden context, not a same-year or traffic-exposure risk rate. Black-spot accident reference periods and rectification reply dates are separate. Missing historical observations are omitted rather than connected as zero.

Correlations require declared compatible entities, metric definitions, period basis and aggregation. Sparse overlaps and unverified manual samples are suppressed. Pearson or Spearman association never establishes the effect of highways on economic output, safety or project delivery.
