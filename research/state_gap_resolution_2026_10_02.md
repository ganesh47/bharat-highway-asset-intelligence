# State road-finance gap resolution

Research cutoff: **2 October 2026**. This audit accounts for all **36 current states and union territories**. It supersedes extraction-pending statuses in the [original discovery audit](state_publication_audit_2026_10_02.md), which remains unchanged as historical retrieval evidence.

Verified CAG road-capital disclosures now cover **30 jurisdictions**: five previously extracted, 21 newly completed indexed accounts, and Rajasthan/Jammu and Kashmir/Delhi/Puducherry recovered through regional or audit-report publications. There are **93 disclosed observations**, of which **89 are calculation-eligible**. These are mixed years and scopes, including four Rajasthan SH subset rows; they are not a total-expenditure measure.

Current FY2024–25 revenue is available for **28 jurisdictions**. Current capital is disclosed for 30, but only 28 have eligible values because Assam and Manipur contain unreconciled totals. Prior FY2023–24 capital comparisons cover 29. Delhi and Puducherry currently provide CAG audit-report capital narratives only; their head 3054 revenue remains unavailable. Puducherry also lacks a prior-year road-capital comparative.

Assam publishes current capital of **₹9,307.45 crore** in its functional statement and **₹9,307.46 crore** in its capital statement. Manipur publishes **₹617.35 crore** and **₹617.36 crore**. All four values retain their own statement and page citations, remain visible as disclosures, and are excluded from calculations. No value is chosen or synthetically corrected.

Rajasthan SH minor head 03 is a subset of the major-head total. Its negative net revenue amounts follow published receipts/recoveries footnotes; original INR lakh amounts are retained and normalized to crore. Jammu and Kashmir uses the present UT boundary and excludes pre-31 October 2019 unapportioned cumulative balances. Tamil Nadu head 5054 retains its stated investment/assistance scope. Nagaland’s reported ₹46.30 crore classification caveat is not assigned to an unidentified 3054 cell.

Goa’s alternate FY2024–25 road actuals remain **retrieval-blocked and under investigation**. The empty central CAG index is a discovery limitation, not proof that no official publication exists. The [Goa official budget portal](https://goabudget.gov.in/) and its [FY2026–27 speech candidate](https://goabudget.gov.in/assets/documents/2026-27/BudgetSpeech_2026-2027.pdf) have produced timeout/TLS retrieval failures. No road amount or publication day is admitted from search snippets. Verified budget actuals would retain a distinct assurance and statement basis.

The five remaining nonlegislative UTs—Andaman and Nicobar Islands, Chandigarh, merged Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu, Ladakh, and Lakshadweep—have no admitted comparable head-specific road actuals in these families. Ladakh’s alternate UT selector contains no annual account file; Chandigarh’s contains monthly accounts. The [Puducherry annual-account index](https://dat.py.gov.in/annual-accounts) lists only through FY2023–24; its newer CAG audit report supplies the admitted current capital narrative. Union appropriation accounts are a different fiscal scope; absence of a state budget never means zero expenditure.

All annual FY2024–25 observations retain **31 March 2025** as their cutoff; FY2023–24 comparisons retain **31 March 2024**. Unknown publication days remain unknown. Puducherry’s whole-crore capital narrative retains its rounding and capital-classification caveat. Source hashes, CSV hashes, metric availability and individual gaps are recorded in the [machine-readable resolution](state_gap_resolution_2026_10_02.json).
