# Completion of indexed state road-finance accounts

Research cutoff: 2 October 2026. This extension closes extraction of all 21 FY2024–25 Finance Accounts publications identified as pending in the state publication audit. It adds 65 cited disclosures, of which 61 are calculation eligible. Together with the existing five state sources, the audited-account collection now covers 26 states; the separate alternate-portal module and territory review are outside this extension.

The figures are audited state-government cash-account flows as reported, covering functional Roads and Bridges across road classes. They are not State Highway-only expenditure, corporation accounts, corridor costs or state corporation debt. Revenue comes from Statement 4A and capital from major head 5054 in Statement 5. Head 5055 Road Transport and progressive expenditure are kept separate. Historical FY2023–24 capital comparatives retain 31 March 2024 cutoffs; FY2024–25 annual actuals retain 31 March 2025. No publication day is inferred from filenames, certificate signatures, document metadata, index timestamps or retrieval.

† Assam Statement 4A reports current capital ₹9,307.45 crore while Statement 5 reports ₹9,307.46 crore. Manipur reports ₹617.35 crore and ₹617.36 crore respectively. Both cells from each publication remain visible as distinct metric records, and all four current-capital observations are excluded from calculations pending reconciliation. The ₹0.01 crore differences are not corrected, rounded away or assigned to a balancing item.

All 42 relevant statement pages were rendered and visually reviewed. Nagaland and Punjab tables required scanned-page review; Arunachal Pradesh, Kerala, Tamil Nadu and other capital tables use rotated layouts. Tamil Nadu and Kerala duplicate facing-page text is counted once. Uttarakhand puts current-year columns before prior-year columns; labels, rather than a global column position, determine the period. Andhra Pradesh’s separate allocated amount and bold unapportioned legacy cumulative balances are excluded from annual flows.

Tamil Nadu’s published MH5054 footnote includes transport finance/capital assistance items. These remain within its as-reported head total, with the qualification attached; the total is not recast as pure construction. Nagaland Statement 4B printed page 23 reports a ₹46.30 crore Central Road Infrastructure Fund transfer misclassified under Object Head 13 Major Works. It does not identify a separate MH3054 cell for that transfer, so no attribution or synthetic reclassification is made.

Provenance is stored in `data/raw/manual/evidence/cag_state_accounts_completion_reviewed_tables.json`. The module pins that review bundle and each original PDF with SHA-256, preserves original INR crore values and records one-based physical plus printed page references. Native text/OCR discovery fixtures preserve difficult page contents but are not independently admitted as numerical evidence. Downloaded PDFs remain in the raw evidence archive; hashes and governed CSVs are versioned. A changed PDF or reviewed-cell bundle fails closed until re-reviewed. A missing local document preserves an explicit extraction gap rather than publishing zeros or deleting an earlier validated extract.

Reproduction: `python -m pipelines.state_accounts_completion --download --raw-root data/raw` retrieves the verified pinned documents with bounded TLS requests. `python -m pipelines.state_accounts_completion --raw-root data/raw --research-cutoff 2026-10-02` rebuilds only this extension’s governed snapshots. Integration uses `DOCUMENTS`, `SOURCE_IDS`, `document_path` and `extend_snapshots`; the shared core registers sources centrally.
