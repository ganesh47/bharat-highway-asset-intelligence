Ganga Expressway Entrance Controlled Project (Green Field) Updated Status

For Omni-directional development of Uttar Pradesh, construction of fully access-controlled Ganga Expressway is proposed starting from  N.H. 334, in district Meerut and ending in district Prayagraj. The foundation stone of this expressway has been completed on 18th December, 2021 by Hon'ble Prime Minister in district Shahjahanpur.

Ending point of the project:- At Prayagraj bye pass of (N.H.-2) in district Prayagraj.

Length of project:- 594 KM approx.

Districts benefited from the project:- Meerut, Hapur, Bulandshahar, Amroha, Sambhal, Badaun, Shahjahanpur, Hardoi, Unnao, Rai Bareli, Pratapgarh, and Prayagraj.

Width of expressway – This expressway will be of 06 lanes (expandable up to 08 lanes) and all the structures will be constructed of 08 lane width. Width of ROW (Right of Way) of the expressway is proposed as 120M, service road  of 3.75 M width will be constructed at one side of the expressway in the staggered form so that residents of nearby villages of the project area may get smooth transportation facility.

The inauguration of Ganga Expressway was successfully completed on 29.April .2026 by Hon'ble Prime Minister Narendra Modi at Bilgram tehsil, Village Shyampur, district-Hardoi .
